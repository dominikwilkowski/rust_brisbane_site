### What the Tokio Is Going On?

*A practical guide to debugging async Rust*
Async Rust is great until something stops making progress.
Your app is running, nothing has crashed, but tasks aren't finishing,
latency is climbing, or CPU usage is through the roof. Where do you even start?

Tokio Console gives you a live view inside the Tokio runtime,
showing what your tasks are doing, what they're waiting on, and where things are going wrong.

In this practical, demo-driven talk, we'll instrument a Rust application,
explore Tokio Console, and hunt down blocked tasks, excessive polling, and resource contention.
We'll also look at how to turn those insights into real performance improvements.
